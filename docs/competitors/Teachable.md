# Teachable - Deep Technical Audit & Market Intelligence (2024)

## 1. Meta Data & Market Positioning

- **Primary Keyword:** Simple online course creation platform
- **Target Audience:** Solopreneurs, beginners, and educators who want a hassle-free, out-of-the-box solution to host video courses.
- **Value Proposition:** The easiest way to turn your knowledge into a business. Beautiful courses, zero design skills required.
- **Brand Resonance:** Accessible, Modern, Creator-First.

## 2. Pricing & Transaction Fees Anatomy (2024 Strategy)

- **Free Plan:** $0/mo. Massive catch: $1 + 10% transaction fee per sale.
- **Basic Plan:** $59/mo (or $39/mo annually). 5% transaction fee per sale.
- **Pro Plan:** $159/mo (or $119/mo annually). 0% transaction fees.
- **Pro+ Plan:** $249/mo (or $199/mo annually). 0% transaction fees.
- **Business Plan:** $665/mo (or $499/mo annually).
- **The "Tax" Model:** Teachable's business model relies heavily on taxing beginners. A creator on the $59/mo Basic plan selling $5,000/mo pays $250 in transaction fees. They effectively force successful users to upgrade to the $159/mo Pro plan.

## 3. Technical Specs & Feature Architecture

- **Course Builder:** Highly intuitive drag-and-drop builder focusing heavily on curriculum structure (videos, quizzes, PDFs).
- **Checkout & Taxes:** Excellent integrated checkout system (Teachable Payments/BackOffice) that handles EU VAT and creator taxes automatically—a massive pain point relief.
- **AI Integrations:** Recently added AI course outline generators and AI quiz generators built directly into the curriculum editor.
- **Marketing:** Basic coupons, order bumps, and affiliates. Weak email marketing compared to Kajabi or Podia.
- **Community:** Basic student commenting features, but lacks a true standalone community forum like Circle or Skool.

## 4. Jackpots of Success (Why They Win)

- **Frictionless Onboarding:** You can sign up and publish a course in under an hour. The UX is incredibly refined for non-technical users.
- **The Free Tier Trap:** By offering a Free plan, they capture massive market share of aspiring creators. Even though the 10% fee is high, beginners don't care because they have zero upfront risk.
- **Tax Handling:** Teachable BackOffice acting as the Merchant of Record (handling global tax compliance) is a massive selling point for international creators.

  3. Vast knowledge base answering specific setup queries ("how to add a video in teachable").

## 6. Strategic Weaknesses (Where Comparlify Can Strike)

- **The 5% Fee on Basic:** This is their Achilles heel. Our ROI calculator can aggressively highlight that Thinkific's $49/mo plan has 0% fees compared to Teachable's $59/mo + 5% fees.
- **Rigid Customization:** The website builder is very basic. It's difficult to make a Teachable site look unique without custom CSS.
- **Community Gap:** As the market shifts toward cohort-based courses and communities (like Skool), Teachable's static video-first approach is aging.
