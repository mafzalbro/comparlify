# 🕵️ Deep Dive Competitor Profile: EmailVendorSelection (EVS)

## 1. Executive Summary

Founded by Jordie van Rijn, EVS is a highly trusted, independent B2B review platform. While their name implies a focus purely on email marketing, they are a major player in CRM, Marketing Automation, and Online Course Platform reviews. They are the "analyst firm" of the affiliate marketing world.

## 2. Technical Stack & Architecture

- **CMS:** WordPress.
- **Review Mechanics:** They utilize a highly structured "Triple Review" process. Their content is not just written by freelancers; it goes through rigorous editorial checks by industry veterans.
- **Monetization:** A mix of traditional affiliate links, lead generation (selling qualified B2B leads to vendors), and sponsored placements.

## 3. SEO & Traffic Analysis (The "Jackpot")

They target B2B buyers, agencies, and established course creators looking to upgrade their tech stack. Their traffic is lower volume but extremely high intent.

3.  **"Best Online Course Platforms"** (Specifically highlighting Teachable, Thinkific, and Kajabi through a marketing lens).

## 4. Why They Win (Their Secret Sauce)

- **Unimpeachable Authority:** They have been doing this since 2009. They have Mor Mester (Head of Content) who has personally tested over 100 tools. Their "Triple Review" methodology builds immense trust.
- **The "Stack" Perspective:** Because they originated in Email/CRM, they review LMS platforms based on how well they integrate with a creator's _existing_ marketing stack.
