# 🕵️ Deep Dive Competitor Profile: Learning Revolution (learningrevolution.net)

## 1. Executive Summary

Founded by Jeff Cobb, Learning Revolution targets a higher-tier audience than solo creators. They focus on training businesses, associations, and enterprise B2B e-learning. Their content is highly strategic rather than purely tactical.

## 2. Technical Stack & Architecture

- **Original CMS:** Ning.com platform (community/website builder).
- **Current Infrastructure:** Likely transitioned to a robust WordPress setup to handle their massive content library of podcasts, webinars, and long-form guides.
- **Traffic Focus:** Highly reliant on Google Search (68% of their traffic), specifically targeting high-value B2B educational queries.

## 3. SEO & Traffic Analysis (The "Jackpot")

Their traffic is smaller in volume than a mass-market review site but much higher in _value_. A single conversion here might be an enterprise Docebo contract rather than a $29/mo Podia subscription.

### The Traffic "Jackpots" (Top Performing Pages)

1.  **"How to Price an Online Course"** (A massive pain point for high-end educators).
2.  **"Choosing an LMS"** (Guides aimed at organizations, not solo creators).
3.  **B2B Platform Reviews:** (Reviews of LearnWorlds, Docebo, Canvas, rather than just Teachable).

## 4. Why They Win (Their Secret Sauce)

- **Deep Subject Matter Authority:** Jeff Cobb wrote the book on this. They aren't affiliate marketers; they are e-learning consultants who happen to use affiliate links.
- **Podcast & Audio:** They build deep parasocial trust through long-form audio interviews with industry leaders.
