# LearnWorlds - Deep Technical Audit & Market Intelligence (2024)

## 1. Meta Data & Market Positioning

- **Primary Keyword:** SCORM-compliant interactive course platform
- **Target Audience:** Professional educators, established training organizations, and B2B enterprise clients who need rigorous educational standards (SCORM) rather than just "marketing funnels".
- **Value Proposition:** The most powerful, customizable, and interactive online learning platform. Build a true online school, not just a checkout page.
- **Brand Resonance:** Academic, Enterprise-Grade, Feature-Rich, "The professional's choice".

## 2. Pricing & Transaction Fees Anatomy (2024 Strategy)

- **Starter Plan:** $29/mo (or $24/mo annually). Massive catch: $5 transaction fee _per course sale_. (This is brutal for low-ticket items).
- **Pro Trainer Plan:** $99/mo (or $79/mo annually). **0% transaction fees.** Unlocks subscriptions, live classes, affiliate management.
- **Learning Center:** $299/mo (or $249/mo annually). Unlocks full white-labeling, API, advanced reporting, and 20 admins.
- **The "High-End Push":** The Starter plan's $5 flat fee makes it unusable for anyone selling a $20 eBook or mini-course. It forces serious creators immediately into the $99/mo Pro Trainer tier.

## 3. Technical Specs & Feature Architecture

- **Course Interactive Engine:** The clear winner in interactive learning. You can add pop-up questions, buttons, and quizzes _directly inside the video player pausing the video until the student interacts_.
- **SCORM Compliance:** Deep, native support for SCORM and HTML5 files (required for corporate B2B training contracts).
- **Website Builder:** Massive, highly customizable block-builder. Much more powerful than Teachable or Kajabi, though slightly steeper learning curve.
- **Assessments & Certificates:** Advanced grading, exam engines, and automated, deeply customizable PDF certificate generation with unique verification IDs.
- **Mobile Apps:** They offer an add-on service to build and publish a fully branded iOS/Android app to the exact specifications of the creator's school.

## 4. Jackpots of Success (Why They Win)

- **The "B2B / Academic" Monopoly:** If a company needs to train 500 employees and track precise completion data for HR compliance, they cannot use Kajabi or Skool. LearnWorlds owns this corporate compliance market.
- **Interactive Video:** The native ability to turn a passive video into an interactive assessment without needing third-party tools like H5P or PlayPosit.
- **White-labeling:** At the Learning Center tier, all LearnWorlds branding is completely stripped, making it look like a custom-built enterprise LMS.

## 6. Strategic Weaknesses (Where Comparlify Can Strike)

- **The $5 Flat Fee on Starter:** Our ROI calculator should absolutely nuke this plan. If you sell fifty $20 courses a month on Starter ($1,000 revenue), you pay $250 in fees. You are literally bleeding money compared to Thinkific's $49/mo (0% fee).
- **Complexity and Overwhelm:** The backend is incredibly dense. For a creator who just wants to upload 5 MP4s and get a checkout link, LearnWorlds is intimidating and overkill.
- **Marketing Stack:** While they have basic funnels, their primary focus is education, not marketing. High-ticket creators often have to bolt on external marketing tools.
