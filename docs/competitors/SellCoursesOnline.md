# 🕵️ Deep Dive Competitor Profile: SellCoursesOnline.com

## 1. Executive Summary

SellCoursesOnline.com is a dominant affiliate review hub for course creators. They are not a software company; they are a media company monetizing through incredibly thorough, unbiased SaaS reviews and platform comparisons.

## 2. Technical Stack & Architecture

- **CMS:** WordPress (Customized Theme).
- **Performance:** highly optimized for Core Web Vitals (crucial for their ranking). They use aggressive caching and lightweight CSS to ensure long-form reviews load instantly.
- **Review Mechanics:** They likely use custom WordPress Custom Post Types (CPTs) and Advanced Custom Fields (ACF) to structure their 60-point feature breakdowns consistently across all reviews.

## 3. SEO & Traffic Analysis (The "Jackpot")

Their entire business model relies on Organic Search (SEO).

### The Traffic "Jackpots" (Top Performing Pages)

Their highest-converting and most trafficked pages are almost exclusively bottom-of-the-funnel commercial intent pages:

1.  **"Best Online Course Platforms"** (The massive roundup post, updated annually).
2.  **"Kajabi vs Teachable"** (The industry's most hotly debated matchup).

## 4. Why They Win (Their Secret Sauce)

- **"Show, Don't Tell" Methodology:** They don't just read the marketing pages of Kajabi; they actually buy the software, build a test course, and screenshot the entire process. This builds unshakeable trust.
- **Exhaustive Depth:** Their reviews are often 5,000+ words. Google favors this comprehensive topical authority.
